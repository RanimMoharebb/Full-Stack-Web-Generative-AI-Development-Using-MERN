Our Story

A Legacy Born on the Mediterranean

In 1995, Chef Hassan Khalil opened a small shawarma stand on the corniche of Alexandria with a dream — to share the authentic flavors of his grandmother's kitchen with the world. What started as a humble cart has grown into Alexandria's most beloved shawarma destination.

Our secret lies in the marinade — a blend of 15 hand-ground spices passed down through three generations, combined with the freshest local produce from Alexandria's morning markets. Every bite carries the soul of the Mediterranean.
